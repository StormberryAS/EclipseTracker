First Android release. EclipseTracker lists every solar and lunar eclipse to the end of 2100 from NASA's catalogue, counts down to the next one, and sets alarms for any of them: on the day or up to a week before at a time you choose, or up to 2 hours before greatest eclipse. The phone rings them itself, over the lock screen, or hands a calendar file to your calendar app. Nine permissions, all for the alarms. No internet access. A functioning prototype from Stormberry AS.
